Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Coatlio helps you measure rooms and estimate paint and tiles. This policy describes how the current app handles information. The app is provided by the developer identified on its App Store product page.

1. Information processed on your device

Camera images and spatial information are processed on your device for camera previews, measurements and room models. Project names, dimensions, saved models, material estimates and preferences are stored locally in the app’s storage. Scanning features may also create temporary files.

The current app requires no account, has no app-owned backend and does not automatically upload room data to the developer. It does not provide its own cloud synchronization. Device backup services may include app data, depending on your backup settings.

2. Camera and permissions

Camera access enables the live camera view and camera-based measurement. Supported room scanning also uses spatial information from Apple’s ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks. You can deny or revoke camera access in iOS Settings. Camera features will then be unavailable, while manual dimension entry and saved projects remain accessible. The app does not request microphone access.

3. Files you export or share

PDF, CSV and 3D model exports can contain project names, room dimensions, layouts and material estimates. You choose where to save or send them using the system file or sharing interface. Files in the app’s Documents storage can also be accessible through system file sharing.

Recipients and services such as iCloud Drive or email handle exported copies under their own policies. Export only information you are entitled to share. Deleting the app does not delete copies already exported.

4. Optional usage analytics and remote configuration

Coatlio uses Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) to understand which features work well and where people encounter problems. Usage analytics is off by default. It starts only after you enable Usage analytics in Settings, and is unavailable if Firebase is not configured. You can use measurements, saved projects, exports and purchases without agreeing to analytics. Accepting these Terms or Apple’s tracking permission does not turn analytics on.

When enabled, the app sends events about screens visited; onboarding and tutorial steps; buttons, menus and dialogs used; camera or tracking permission outcomes; scan start, readiness, completion, cancellation and failure; calculator field edits and completion; paint or tile selections and calculation saves; saved-project filters, selection and rename actions; export stages, file format and outcome; preferences; and opening a policy link. A rename event records that renaming happened, not the project name. A field-edit event records which field changed, not the room dimension entered. Export events describe the export action, not the exported file or its contents. Errors use fixed categories rather than raw error messages.

Event details include fixed screen and control names, feature or material categories, tutorial step numbers and selected settings or calculator options. Some options include numeric choices, such as paint coats, coverage presets, reserve percentage, wall-coverage percentage, standard tile sizes and tiles per box; paint container size is grouped as small, medium, large or another saved option. These are calculation choices, not measured room dimensions, room geometry, wall identifiers or final material quantities.

Google also processes SDK-generated activity such as first launch, sessions, engagement and app/OS updates, together with app version, device model, operating system, language, event times and a pseudonymous app-instance identifier. Google receives network requests containing an IP address and may derive approximate location under its service practices; the app does not request GPS or precise location access. Where supported by the SDK’s purchase measurement, automatic in-app purchase events may include product information, price, currency and quantity. The app does not manually send StoreKit transaction identifiers through its custom tracking events.

The current integration does not collect IDFA or IDFV, set an account/user ID, enable advertising personalization, or send camera images, room scans, project names, room dimensions, typed free text, file names, file paths, exported contents or contact details in custom events. The app currently displays no third-party advertising and does not link this information with other companies’ apps or websites for advertising. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency choice is separate from Usage analytics. The developer does not sell room data.

Turning Usage analytics off stops further analytics collection and resets the analytics data and app-instance identifier stored by the SDK on this device. App-owned events that occurred without consent are discarded, rather than saved for later replay. Withdrawal does not automatically erase data already processed by Google. Analytics retention and deletion depend on the developer’s Google Analytics property settings and Google’s applicable policies; resetting analytics is not a deletion request for Firebase Remote Config or Apple purchase records. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy for Google’s data handling.

Firebase Remote Config separately downloads the free-scan allowance, product identifiers and paywall settings, and can listen for configuration updates. This service operates independently of the Usage analytics switch. It uses a Firebase installation identifier and technical metadata such as app and SDK versions, operating system, language/country code and time zone; network requests disclose your IP address to Google. It does not upload room data or the locally stored free-scan count. Turning analytics off does not stop configuration requests or delete the Firebase installation identifier. Under Firebase’s published retention rules, that identifier is retained until deletion is requested through Firebase’s installation-deletion mechanism; this app does not currently offer that mechanism in Settings. Configuration values are cached on the device and bundled defaults are used when no valid downloaded value is available.

5. Storage, deletion and your choices

Saved projects remain in local storage until the files or the app are deleted. To remove the app’s local documents, preferences and free-scan counter, use Delete App in iOS Settings; Offload App may retain documents and data. Manage backups and exported copies separately in their respective services or folders. Deleting the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase records held by Apple, Google, GitHub or an export recipient.

You can withdraw optional analytics consent in Coatlio Settings and change camera or Apple tracking permissions in iOS Settings. Withdrawal, retention and Firebase installation identifiers are described in section 4. Where applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent, contact the developer using the channel below. The developer may need enough information to locate pseudonymous records and cannot identify an installation from a room scan or project name that was never uploaded. Withdrawing consent does not change processing that lawfully occurred earlier.

The developer cannot access, recover or remotely delete room files stored only on your device. Keep copies of important work and protect access to your device.

6. Information you send for support

If you contact the developer, the contact details, message and attachments you choose to send may be used to answer your request and resolve the issue. Support information is kept as needed for that purpose and any applicable legal obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of support information through the same contact channel, subject to applicable law. You do not need to send a room scan to ask for help.

7. Purchases and payment information

When available, Coatlio Pro uses Apple In-App Purchase for weekly subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime unlock. Apple handles billing and payment details under its own privacy policy. StoreKit verifies signed transactions on your device to determine current access, expiry or revocation. The app processes product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, and entitlement status for that purpose; it does not receive your full payment card number or send transaction records to a separate purchase server. Recognized product IDs and the last verified access status are stored locally; access is rechecked with StoreKit. Optional Firebase purchase measurement, where supported, is described in section 4 and is separate from the StoreKit checks needed to provide purchased access.

Successful free room scans are counted locally on this installation. Cancelled or failed scans do not use a free scan. This count is not synchronized across devices and may be reset by deleting app data. Payment, entitlement checks and room measurement do not require analytics consent or advertising tracking permission.

8. Changes and contact

The revision date above identifies this policy. If the app’s data practices change, the policy and relevant App Store disclosures will be updated, and permissions will be requested where required. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer’s contact details available through App Support on Coatlio’s App Store product page.

9. This website

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not set its own cookies or load analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts or embedded tracking services. Reading these pages sends normal web requests to the hosting provider, which may process technical information such as IP addresses for its operations and security.

See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the hosting provider’s practices.
